Rules

Do & don't

Do

  • Default to the dark canvas; use green as a single accent.
  • Sentence case for UI; UPPERCASE only for overlines/status.
  • Tabular numerals in tables and KPIs.
  • Tight radii — 4/6/8/12px.

Don't

  • Flood large areas with vivid green or the gradient.
  • Use pill-shaped CTAs, or emoji in product/reports.
  • Switch type families for hierarchy.
  • Recolor or stretch the logo.
